Sports

Soccer

I played for the IIIS soccer team throughout my undergraduate years. I started as a defender before moving to goalkeeper, where my quick reflexes were a better fit. The team improved its results in each of those four years; in my final year, I kept clean sheets throughout the group stage of the university-wide cup.

I do not support a particular professional soccer club. I have not played recently, but would like to return to the sport.

Baseball

I began following baseball more closely in 2024 and support the Los Angeles Dodgers. My favorite player is Yoshinobu Yamamoto, whom I have followed since he won three consecutive Sawamura Awards in Japan.

I have attended games at approximately six MLB stadiums and generally plan to see the Dodgers when visiting Los Angeles or when they play at Fenway Park. My own playing experience is limited to a one-semester undergraduate course and occasional batting-cage practice.

Games

The game genres I play most often are:

  • Factory-building and automation: Factorio, Satisfactory, and Dyson Sphere Program.
  • Strategic roguelikes: decision-heavy games including The Binding of Isaac, Slay the Spire, and Balatro.
  • Puzzle games: games centered on discovering indirect rules, including Outer Wilds, Superliminal, and Patrick's Parabox.

Artists

HIMEHINA

HIMEHINA are my kami-oshi, and I have followed them since 2021. I have attended their concerts in Japan, China, and the United States, beginning with their 2023 summer performance in Saitama. Later events included their seventh-anniversary concerts in Tokyo and tour performances in Yokohama and San Jose. I also own a HIMEHINA-themed itasha.

Other favorites

Todoroki Hajime of ReGLOSS is my second-favorite performer, and I hope to attend a ReGLOSS concert in the future. Other favorites include Pmaru, AZKi, KAF, Ado, other Hololive talents, Gakuen Idolmaster, and Nornis.
